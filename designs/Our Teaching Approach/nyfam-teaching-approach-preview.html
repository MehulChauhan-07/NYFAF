The Combined Teaching Advantage

Technical injection training meets structural anatomy.

NYFAF’s dual-instructor approach brings together injection technique, facial structure, vascular awareness, treatment planning, and post-care considerations so participants can view each procedure as a complete clinical decision—not an isolated injection step.

Structural Foundation

Facial anatomy and patient structure guide treatment planning.

Technical Delivery

Technique is demonstrated, practiced, corrected, and discussed in context.

Total Competence

Assessment, execution, documentation, and post-care are taught as one workflow.

What You Will Learn

Comprehensive Curriculum in Aesthetic Injectables

The curriculum builds a clinical foundation for safe, supervised injectable training. Exact depth and treatment areas vary by the program selected.

Consultation, documentation, ethical practice, patient communication, and responsible professional growth.

Clinical Eligibility & Training Standards

Important Information Before You Enroll

NYFAF programs are designed for licensed medical professionals. Participation in hands-on activities may vary according to the selected course, professional license, experience level, applicable regulations, and clinical considerations.

badge

Participants must provide an active professional license or other required credentials.

inventory_2

All live models, injectable products, and clinical materials are provided or approved by NYFAF.

block

Outside models and products are not permitted for safety and liability reasons.

gavel

Participants must follow the scope-of-practice requirements that apply in their state, country, or professional jurisdiction.

workspace_premium

A Certificate of Completion recognizes participation in the educational program and does not replace professional licensure or expand an individual’s legal scope of practice.
