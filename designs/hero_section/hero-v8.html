Top Questions Only

Homepage FAQ

Keep only the objections that directly affect enrollment; move the rest to the full FAQ and International pages.

Who is eligible to attend?+

NYFAF training is designed for licensed medical professionals including physicians, dentists, nurse practitioners, physician assistants, and registered nurses. Participants remain responsible for complying with their professional scope of practice.

Is the training hands-on?+

Yes. The live site describes supervised injections on live models as a core part of the training experience.

Which course should I choose?+

Basic is designed for first-time injectors, Comprehensive is an intensive two-day full-face program, and Advanced is intended for practitioners with prior injectable training or experience.

What is included in the course fee?+

The current site lists didactic sessions, hands-on training, live-model practice, training materials, Certificate of Completion, and post-training mentorship access.

Is the course accredited or CME approved?+

The live FAQ says CME availability may vary and recommends contacting NYFAF for current accreditation details. The site separately describes a continuing-education collaboration with Vizstara Professional.
