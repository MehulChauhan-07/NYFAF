Who We Are

A Mentorship Model, Not Just a Course Day

NYFAM was built around a straightforward idea: injectable education should help a clinician understand why a treatment plan makes sense, not only memorize where an injection goes.

Training connects foundational knowledge with patient-specific assessment, instructor demonstration, supervised clinical experience, and feedback. Basic, Advanced, and Comprehensive courses allow professionals to choose a level that matches their current experience rather than forcing every learner into the same pathway.

The public-facing NYFAM brand operates as New York Facial Aesthetics Mentorship under New York Facial Aesthetics Forum (NYFAF), with training delivered in New York City.
