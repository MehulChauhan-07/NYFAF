    Why Comprehensive

    Learn individual areas first. Then learn to plan the face as a whole.

    The Comprehensive Difference

    Shift from filling a feature to planning the face.

    At the comprehensive level, treatment is no longer organized around a single syringe or a single facial area. Participants learn to evaluate structural support, volume loss, profile relationships, product characteristics, tissue plane, sequence and how one treatment decision changes the balance of the rest of the face.

    01 · Structure

    Assess support before volume

    Identify structural deficiency, soft-tissue change and treatment priorities before choosing product or technique.

    02 · Product

    Match filler to the goal

    Use filler characteristics, tissue behavior and anatomical area to guide product selection and placement.

    03 · Sequence

    Treat in the right order

    Plan staged or multi-area treatment based on proportion, structural support, volume needs and patient goals.

    04 · Balance

    See the face as one system

    Coordinate midface, lips, chin, jawline, folds and profile relationships without overcorrection.

    Course FAQ

    Questions about the Comprehensive Dermal Filler pathway

    Is Comprehensive Dermal Filler different from Basic Filler training? add

    Yes. Basic focuses on the foundational product, anatomy, consultation and injection framework. Comprehensive connects those skills with advanced vascular anatomy, product strategy, structural planning, full-face balancing and more complex clinical decision-making.

    Does the program cover both needle and cannula techniques? add

    The curriculum includes needle-versus-cannula selection and technique concepts, including depth, direction, product placement and volume control, with supervised clinical application where permitted.

    What treatment areas are included? add

    The pathway includes lips, perioral area, cheeks, midface, nasolabial folds, marionette lines, chin, jawline, pre-jowl region, profile balancing and multi-area facial planning.

    Is complication management included? add

    Yes. The curriculum includes vascular awareness, recognition of vascular compromise, hyaluronidase principles, nodules, infection considerations, documentation, follow-up and appropriate escalation.

    Is there support after the course? add

    The program content includes one year of continuing mentorship focused on assessment, product selection, treatment planning, technique and clinical decision-making.
