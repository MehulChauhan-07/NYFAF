Comprehensive Course FAQ

Questions specific to this program.

General admissions, international travel and policy questions should remain on their dedicated pages.

Do I need to complete the Basic course first?add

NYFAF currently requires either completion of a Basic Botox training course or documented prior neuromodulator experience. The live page also states participants should be actively injecting and comfortable with upper-face treatments.

Why are Botox and filler on one page?add

They are currently sold as one two-day Comprehensive program with one enrollment and one published fee. The page separates the two clinical focuses inside the same program so visitors understand the curriculum without mistaking them for separate products.

Will I inject live models?add

The current course page describes supervised injection practice on pre-screened live models as part of both the clinical format and hands-on sessions.

Which filler areas are included?add

The published curriculum includes lips, cheeks and midface, chin and jawline, nasolabial folds, facial volume restoration and full-face consultation/planning. The detailed Day 2 schedule specifically mentions hands-on work with lips and nasolabial folds.

What happens after the course?add

The live Comprehensive page currently advertises one year of complimentary mentorship, including the ability to bring patients to the center for guided treatment and clinical decision support. Confirm current terms directly with NYFAF before enrollment.
