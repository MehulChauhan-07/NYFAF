Enrollment & Eligibility
Where is NYFAF training held?+

The current site says training is hosted in Midtown Manhattan, New York City, inside Diamond District Aesthetics at 10 W 46th St Suite 1401.

Who is the training designed for?+

NYFAF lists licensed medical professionals including MDs, DOs, dentists, nurse practitioners, physician assistants and registered nurses. Participants remain responsible for professional scope-of-practice requirements.

What are the enrollment requirements?+

The live FAQ says participants should hold an active medical license, be legally authorized to perform injectables, and provide professional credentials. Foundational courses do not require prior injectable experience.

Training Experience
Is the course hands-on?+

Yes. The current site describes supervised live-model injections as a core component of its Midtown Manhattan training.

What makes NYFAF different from larger injector courses?+

The live site emphasizes small-group instruction, direct supervision, live-model opportunities, real clinic workflow experience, and post-training mentorship.

Do you provide post-training mentorship?+

The site says participants may receive clinical case discussion support, complication-management assistance, business-development guidance and continued mentorship access.

Courses & Inclusions
Do you offer beginner and advanced tracks?+

Yes. NYFAF currently lists Basic, Comprehensive and Advanced BOTOX® & Dermal Filler training programs for different experience levels.

What is included in the course fee?+

The live FAQ currently lists didactic lecture sessions, hands-on training, live-model practice, training materials, Certificate of Completion and post-training mentorship access.

Accreditation & Certificates
What certificate do participants receive?+

The current site says participants receive a Certificate of Completion recognizing participation in the educational training program.

Is the course CME-approved or accredited?+

The live FAQ says CME availability may vary and asks visitors to contact NYFAF for current accreditation details. Elsewhere, the site describes continuing-education delivery in collaboration with Vizstara Professional.

Travel
Can out-of-state providers travel to Manhattan for training?+

Yes. NYFAF says many providers travel to its Midtown Manhattan location from the region and from out of state.

Still deciding which course is right?

Use the course comparison on the homepage or speak with admissions for current dates, prerequisites and continuing-education details.
