Visa & Documentation

Support for the purpose of your training visit

The NYFAM site says many international participants apply for a B-1/B-2 visitor visa for short-term professional training and tells applicants to verify country-specific requirements with their local U.S. embassy or consulate.

Important: This page should not promise a visa category or approval. Visa decisions are made by U.S. authorities. Applicants should confirm the appropriate visa/status for their individual circumstances. This is general admissions information, not immigration advice.

International Questions

International applicant FAQ

Is a NYFAF certificate automatically recognized in my country?+

No automatic recognition should be assumed. The current NYFAM FAQ tells participants to follow local medical regulations and verify requirements with their local medical board.

What visa should I use?+

NYFAF currently says many international participants apply for a B-1/B-2 visitor visa for short professional training, but applicants should verify their own circumstances with the U.S. embassy or consulate.

Does NYFAF guarantee visa approval?+

No. The current site explicitly states that visa approval decisions are made independently by U.S. embassies and consulates.

Does NYFAF arrange hotels or airport transfers?+

The site says it provides hotel recommendations and general travel guidance. It does not directly arrange airport transfers.

What happens if travel plans change?+

Review the published Refund & Cancellation Policy before purchasing flights or lodging. The policy states that NYFAF is not responsible for travel expenses, lodging costs, lost wages or other incidental expenses.
