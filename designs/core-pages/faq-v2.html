Quick Answers

The questions that affect enrollment most.

Do I need prior experience?

Not for foundational courses. Advanced training is intended for practitioners with prior training or equivalent experience.

Is the training hands-on?

Yes. NYFAF describes supervised live-model injection as a core part of its clinical training.

Where is training held?

Midtown Manhattan at 10 W 46th St Suite 1401, New York, NY 10036.

Do international applicants attend?

Yes. NYFAF has a separate international applicant process and provides training-related support documents after enrollment.

01 · Enrollment & Eligibility

Can I attend NYFAF training?

Who is NYFAF training designed for?add

The current NYFAF site lists licensed medical professionals including physicians, dentists, nurse practitioners, physician assistants and registered nurses. Participants are responsible for meeting the professional and legal requirements that apply to them.

What are the enrollment requirements?add

NYFAF states that participants should hold active professional credentials and provide licensing information as requested. Eligibility for hands-on participation may depend on professional role, course level and applicable regulations.

Do I need prior injectable experience?add

Foundational courses are designed for providers with little or no prior injectable experience. Advanced programs are intended for practitioners who already have foundational training or equivalent experience.

Can registered nurses attend?add

Registered nurses are included among NYFAF’s intended participants. Scope of practice, supervision and the ability to perform injectable procedures vary by jurisdiction.

02 · Training Experience

What happens during training?

Is NYFAF training hands-on?add

Yes. The current program describes live-model injections under direct faculty supervision, with treatment planning, technique, safety, patient assessment and complication-awareness built into the clinical experience.

Are live models provided?add

NYFAF states that live models and injection materials are provided for the clinical phase. Outside models and products are not permitted under the current participation clarifications.

What does the dermal filler curriculum cover?add

The live site lists areas including lips, cheek and midface volumization, chin and jawline contouring, nasolabial folds, tear trough correction and full-face aesthetic consultation. Course depth depends on the program selected.

What makes the NYFAF format different from a large seminar?add

NYFAF emphasizes small-group instruction, direct faculty supervision, live-model opportunities, real clinic workflow experience and ongoing post-training mentorship rather than observation-only education.

What is included in the course fee?add

The current site generally lists didactic sessions, supervised hands-on training, live-model practice, course materials, completion documentation and post-training mentorship access. Exact inclusions vary by course.

Certificate & Continuing Education

What recognition do participants receive?

NYFAF currently states that participants receive a Certificate of Completion. The live site also describes continuing-education delivery in collaboration with Vizstara Professional, an ADA CERP recognized provider, and lists 10 continuing-education credits for the activity.

03 · International Applicants

Traveling to New York for training?

Open the full International Applicant Guide →
What visa do international participants usually use?add

NYFAF’s current guidance says many participants apply for the B-1/B-2 visitor category for short professional training. Visa requirements differ by applicant, so participants should verify their circumstances with the appropriate U.S. embassy or consulate.

What visa-support documents can NYFAF provide?add

The current international information lists an invitation letter, enrollment confirmation, course schedule or agenda, payment receipt and training-location verification.

Does NYFAF guarantee visa approval?add

No. Visa decisions are made independently by U.S. government authorities.

Does NYFAF help with hotels or airport transportation?add

NYFAF provides hotel recommendations, airport-arrival guidance, transportation tips and commute information. The current site says it does not directly arrange airport transfers.

04 · Payment & Policies

Know the terms before you register.

Policy details should live on the policy pages; the FAQ should summarize the decision-critical points and route visitors to the full terms.

When is enrollment confirmed?add

The published refund policy states that enrollment is confirmed after full payment or an approved payment arrangement.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?add

Cancellation and transfer terms depend on timing and availability. Requests must be submitted in writing and may involve non-refundable fees or future-course credit depending on the circumstances.

What if NYFAF cancels a course?add

The published policy says participants will be offered a full refund or the option to transfer to a future date if NYFAF cancels a course.

Are travel and lodging expenses reimbursed?add

The published refund policy states that NYFAF is not responsible for travel expenses, lodging costs, lost wages or other incidental expenses.
