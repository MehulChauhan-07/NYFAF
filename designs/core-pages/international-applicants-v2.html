Who Can Apply

International training for qualified medical professionals.

NYFAF programs are designed for licensed healthcare professionals. International participants should be able to document their professional credentials and remain responsible for the scope-of-practice and licensing requirements that apply in their home country.

Visa & Documentation

Prepare a clear record of your training visit.

NYFAF’s current international guidance states that many participants use the B-1/B-2 visitor category for short professional training. Applicants should verify the appropriate visa or admission status for their individual circumstances with the U.S. embassy or consulate serving their country.

Important: NYFAF can provide training-related documents, but visa applications and approval decisions remain with the applicant and U.S. authorities.

International Applicant FAQ

Questions before you book your trip.

What visa do international participants usually use?add

NYFAF’s current guidance says many international participants apply for a B-1/B-2 visitor visa for short professional training. Applicants should verify their own visa requirements with the U.S. embassy or consulate serving their country.

Does NYFAF provide visa-support documents?add

The current site lists an official invitation letter, enrollment confirmation, detailed course schedule or agenda, payment receipt and training-location verification among the documents available after enrollment.

Does NYFAF guarantee visa approval?add

No. Visa decisions are made by U.S. government authorities. NYFAF can document the training program but cannot guarantee an application outcome.

Do I need a U.S. medical license to attend?add

NYFAF’s international guidance says participants may attend as qualified medical professionals using credentials from their home country. Hands-on participation must still comply with applicable licensing, supervision and facility requirements.

Is accommodation included in tuition?add

No. Accommodation is separate. NYFAF provides hotel and neighborhood guidance so participants can choose a stay convenient to the Midtown training location.

Should I book flights before my training date is confirmed?add

Confirm your enrollment and training schedule first. NYFAF’s published refund policy states that the organization is not responsible for travel, lodging, lost wages or other incidental expenses.
