    Why Comprehensive

    Start with the fundamentals. Finish with clinical judgment.

    The Comprehensive BOTOX® pathway is designed to connect the skills normally learned at separate stages. Participants first build a safe, organized approach to assessment and foundational upper-face treatment, then progress into more individualized planning, expanded treatment areas, advanced dosing decisions and case-based clinical thinking.

    Best Fit

    Licensed healthcare professionals who want a complete BOTOX® learning path rather than stopping after a one-day foundational course.

    The Comprehensive Difference

    Move from memorized points to whole-face neuromodulator planning.

    Comprehensive training should change how a participant thinks about treatment. The focus shifts from simply knowing where common injection points are to understanding muscle relationships, baseline asymmetry, dose distribution, movement patterns and how multiple treatment areas influence one another.

    01 · Movement

    Read the face at rest and in motion

    Evaluate dominant and compensating muscles instead of treating every patient from the same map.

    02 · Dose

    Individualize distribution

    Use anatomy, muscle strength, prior treatment and goals to guide conservative dosing decisions.

    03 · Balance

    Connect treatment areas

    Understand how upper- and lower-face treatments can be planned together while preserving expression.

    04 · Judgment

    Know when not to follow a pattern

    Use assessment, safety awareness and follow-up findings to adapt the plan responsibly.

    Course FAQ

    Questions about the Comprehensive BOTOX® pathway

    Is Comprehensive BOTOX® different from Basic BOTOX®? add

    Yes. Basic focuses on the foundational framework. Comprehensive is structured as the complete pathway, connecting that foundation with advanced assessment, individualized dosing, expanded treatment areas and more complex clinical decision-making.

    Does the program include live-model training? add

    The course structure includes live demonstrations and supervised hands-on clinical training where permitted by applicable licensing, scope-of-practice, supervision, facility and regulatory requirements.

    What areas are covered? add

    Coverage begins with the glabella, forehead, crow’s feet and brow fundamentals, then expands into areas such as nasal lines, gummy smile, lip flip, chin, mouth-corner, masseter and introductory neck applications.

    Who is the program designed for? add

    The program is intended for licensed healthcare professionals including MD/DO, DDS/DMD, NP, PA and RN participants, subject to applicable professional regulations.

    Is there support after the course? add

    The program content includes one year of continuing mentorship intended to help participants refine assessment, treatment planning, dosing, technique and clinical decision-making.
